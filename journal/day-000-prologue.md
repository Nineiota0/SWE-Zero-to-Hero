## Why I'm doing this 

Hello stranger,

If you're reading this, this means you are viewing my journey and first journal entry where I aim to become a better SWE. The reason why I am starting this journey is because after interviewing with companies like Amazon and Alkira, I found that I did not know much about the fundamentals of SWE, overall systems and how they work together to build a complete software. I realized that there was a gap between being able to get code working and actually understanding the software I am working with and I want to be able to bridge that gap. Instead of moping around and shifting the blame onto something else, this is my initiative to
become a better SWE.
